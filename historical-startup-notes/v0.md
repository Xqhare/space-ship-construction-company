# Turn based

Base is turn based, but with a global clock / timestamp.
Real time games are technically also turn based, the turns are just sub second.
So I would love a way to define the `time scale` or `speed` of the game.
E.g: One turn = 1 second or 1 minute or 1 hour.

So very much like Aurora4X handles things.
